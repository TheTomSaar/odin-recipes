Pasta Al Limone


Description

This is my spaghetti al limone, a fresh pasta dish, quick to make, and yummi for the summer!

Ingredients

  • 70g butter
  • zest of lemon
  • 10g grated garlic cloves
  • fresh lemon juice
  • 500g spaghetti
  • 50g grated parmigiano-reggiano
  • salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Melt butter in a pan
  2. Add the garlic for 30-60s / until fragrant
  3. Cook the pasta as per instructions in a large pot of water with rock salt
  4. Add pasta to the mix
  5. Add cheese, toss until sauce thickens
  6. Season to taste
  7. Serve and enjoy!

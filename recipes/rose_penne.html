Rose Penne


Description

This is my rose penne, a simple and quick savory dish.

Ingredients

  • 25ml olive oil
  • 25g butter
  • chopped medium onion
  • 10g grated garlic cloves
  • 600g tomato passata
  • 250ml thickened cream
  • 500g penne pasta
  • salt, pepper, and spices to taste

Steps

  1. Bring the onion to a golden color in the butter and olive oil in a pan
  2. Add the garlic for 30-60s / until fragrant
  3. Add the passata, thickened cream, beef stock and orzo
  4. Season with spices to taste
  5. Cook the pasta as per instructions in a large pot of water with rock salt
  6. Combine cooked pasta with the sauce
  7. Serve and enjoy!
